# Thank you to the author

Thank you to Faris Chaudhry for making *The Geometry of Projection Heads:
Conditioning, Invariance, and Collapse*, its implementation, and the released arrays
available. Those artifacts make it possible to inspect the equations, reproduce the
geometric calculations, compare exact Hessians with the paper's estimator, and audit
the limits of the empirical evidence.

This repository is intended as a respectful reproduction and audit record. It credits
the original work, distinguishes source-array re-analysis from independent training,
and keeps shortened runs, assumption failures, and excluded cases visible.
